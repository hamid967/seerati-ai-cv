## Required fields for future additions

Every font, icon, image, illustration, animation, sound, data visualization, or generated asset must record its source URL, creator, license, version, local path, intended use, attribution, reviewer, and expiry/revocation notes. Missing license evidence blocks release.

## Rejected asset classes

Paid Webflow templates, cloneable community sites, copied CSS/JS, unlicensed fonts or images, official government marks, city logos, protected landmark renders, and copied marketing text are not accepted. Generated assets must not imitate named brands or recognizable copyrighted templates.
