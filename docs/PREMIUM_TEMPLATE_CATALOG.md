## Acceptance for every template

Each template must remain original, free, watermark-free, Arabic/English, RTL/LTR, A4, selectable-text, link-preserving, one-to-three-page safe, print-safe, and structurally ATS-labelled without a guarantee. Thumbnail and full preview must remain separate; the gallery must not load the PDF engine or render all 24 full resumes on initial load.

Template names are creative product labels, not claims of government endorsement, official sector standards, or employment outcomes.
