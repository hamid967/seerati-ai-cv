# Saudi Content Validation

The content direction is Arabic-first, professional, direct, and understandable across graduate, early-career, government, technology, engineering, healthcare, finance, HR, sales, operations, tourism, nonprofit, and leadership contexts. English copy must remain natural, concise, and semantically consistent.

Approved terminology includes السيرة الذاتية, المسمى المستهدف, الملخص المهني, الإنجازات, مطابقة الوظيفة, توافق ATS, اقتراح الذكاء الاصطناعي, التسجيل اختياري, بيانات الجلسة, and حذف البيانات.

The product must not say “مضمون 100%”, “اجتز جميع أنظمة ATS”, “احصل على وظيفة فورًا”, “معتمد حكوميًا”, or “الأفضل في السعودية” without independent evidence. Any beta comprehension percentage must come from an approved user study; no such study result is claimed in this commit.
