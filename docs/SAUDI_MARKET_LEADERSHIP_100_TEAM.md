# Seerati — Saudi Market Leadership Program

## Mission

Build the most trusted Arabic-first resume and career application platform for the Saudi market: fast, accessible, privacy-aware, evidence-based, ATS-conscious, bilingual, and honest about what its scores can and cannot predict.

The team operates as 10 squads of 10 people. Every squad owns production code, measurable quality criteria, documentation, and a clear hand-off contract with the other squads. The organization is intentionally cross-functional so product, engineering, design, content, QA, security, and operations are present throughout delivery rather than added at the end.

## Product leadership principles

1. **Arabic-first, not Arabic-translated.** RTL, Saudi terminology, Arabic typography and bilingual switching are first-class architecture requirements.
2. **Evidence before confidence.** Scores must explain their inputs. Claims about the user's career must come from the resume or the user's evidence graph.
3. **No fake hiring probability.** Match/readiness/recruiter scores describe document fit and presentation, never the likelihood of getting hired.
4. **Privacy by design.** Minimize sensitive personal data, warn before unnecessary exposure, keep user-owned career data scoped by RLS, and make provenance understandable.
5. **Accessible by default.** Product UI targets WCAG 2.2 practices: keyboard use, focus clarity, contrast, reduced motion, semantic labeling and assistive-technology compatibility.
6. **Premium without being heavy.** 3D/depth belongs to the product experience; exported resumes remain professional, printable and ATS-conscious.
7. **One Career Truth.** Career Twin + Career Facts + Evidence Vault are the canonical foundation for resumes, application packs, cover letters and interview preparation.
8. **Saudi-market aware without unsupported government claims.** The platform can prepare users for professional profiles and application journeys, but it does not claim direct compatibility or integration with a government platform unless such integration actually exists and is tested.
9. **No destructive automation.** Auto Design, Smart Fit, Tailoring and AI suggestions require review/approval and must not silently remove or invent professional content.
10. **Every major feature gets a permanent guard.** Static checks, TypeScript, Build and domain-specific guards are part of the definition of done.

- Saudi Career Readiness engine.
- Privacy-sensitive-data warnings for resumes.
- Saudi contact/location/bilingual/document readiness checks.
- Dedicated readiness surface linked from Resume Studio.
- Permanent CI guard.

### Stage 6B — Job Match Intelligence & Application Readiness

- Explainable requirement extraction.
- Must-have / partial / missing / unverified map.
- Evidence-backed requirement matching.
- Resume variant comparison.
- Unified readiness score combining ATS, recruiter scan, evidence and job alignment without predicting hiring outcomes.

### Stage 6C — Tailoring Studio

- Before/after proposals.
- Approve/reject per change.
- Preserve every factual claim and number.
- Undo/versions.
- Role-specific ordering and keyword placement without keyword stuffing.

### Stage 6D — Saudi Career Passport

- Reusable structured professional profile.
- Copy-ready field groups for external application journeys.
- Arabic/English identity, education, experience and certification normalization.
- Export bundles; no credential scraping and no password collection.

### Stage 6E — Arabic Career Intelligence

- Broader Saudi/Gulf title and sector taxonomy.
- Arabic skill normalization and protected terms.
- Arabic achievement quality checks.
- Bilingual consistency review.
- Saudi graduate and executive guidance modes.

### Stage 6F — Trust, Security & PDPL Hardening

- Remove unnecessary admin raw-read paths from career evidence/facts.

- Account export/delete lifecycle review.
- Sensitive-data scanner and data minimization UX.

### Stage 6G — Accessibility, Performance & Mobile Excellence

- WCAG 2.2 audit.

### Stage 6H — Content, SEO, Analytics & Commercial Readiness

- Funnel/activation analytics with privacy-aware event taxonomy.

## Definition of market-leadership quality

A feature is not complete because it looks polished. It is complete only when:

- Arabic and English paths both work.
- RTL and keyboard navigation work.
- It passes TypeScript, production build and static domain guards.
- It has clear empty/error/loading states.
- It does not expose or invent personal data.
- Its score or recommendation explains why it exists.
- Print/PDF behavior is correct where the resume is involved.
- Mobile behavior is deliberate.
- The user can reverse consequential automated changes.
- Claims about external platforms are factual and appropriately qualified.
