# Saudi Career Review Rubric

Each synthetic resume review is scored from 1 to 5 across content accuracy, professional summary, achievement clarity, target-title fit, Arabic/English quality, Saudi-market contextual fit, non-discrimination, unnecessary-information restraint, AI suggestion usefulness, and ATS note usefulness.

## Safety rules

A reviewer must not recommend adding a national ID, full address, unrelated personal data, fabricated metrics, government affiliation, or an unsupported certification. Advice must remain optional and explain what fact is missing rather than inventing it.

## Review protocol

Two independent reviewers should score each sector sample. Disagreements are recorded and resolved with an adjudication note. Weighted Kappa ≥0.70 and mean quality ≥4/5 are proposed targets, not achieved results. The current Phase 15 commit defines the rubric; it does not claim human-review evidence.
