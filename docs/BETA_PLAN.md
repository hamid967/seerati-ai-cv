# Phase 15 Controlled Saudi Beta Plan

## Gate

No beta begins from the current commit. The first cohort may contain 20–30 Saudi participants only after critical privacy, deletion, security, PDF, browser, and AI-safety gates pass and the owner approves the cohort.

## Cohort

Graduates, employees, job seekers, leaders, Arabic users, English users, and predominantly mobile-first participants. Expansion to 100 participants requires closing P0 defects and reviewing the first cohort evidence.

## Privacy-safe metrics

Collect only aggregate start rate, completion rate, time to first preview/PDF, PDF success, AI accept/reject/undo counts, ATS completion, deletion success, error rate, trust, and satisfaction. Never collect CV text, names, emails, phones, employers, job descriptions, prompts, responses, or uploaded files.

## Proposed acceptance

Completion ≥85%, PDF success ≥98%, zero critical blocking defects, satisfaction ≥4/5, privacy trust ≥4/5, and session deletion success =100%. These are proposed gates and have not been measured.
