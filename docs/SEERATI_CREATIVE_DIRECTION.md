# Seerati Creative Direction — Phase 17

## Three candidate directions

| Direction                 | Strength                                                                                                     | Risk                                                                              | Decision                        |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Saudi Future Professional | Saudi-first without official branding; editorial, calm, scalable, and compatible with Arabic/English and ATS | Requires restraint so the city story does not become decorative                   | **Selected**                    |
| Neo-Riyadh Digital        | Strong tech and AI energy with dark surfaces and data-like accents                                           | Risks neon, dashboard clutter, and weak resume readability                        | Rejected for primary identity   |
| Human Career Atelier      | Warm, editorial, and reassuring with paper-like surfaces                                                     | Risks feeling like a static document editor and under-communicating AI capability | Useful secondary influence only |

## Selected direction

Saudi Future Professional combines the selected strengths: a clear first-screen promise, resume preview as the hero object, warm sand and pearl surfaces, midnight navy and emerald anchors, abstract city patterns, transparent privacy, and product states that show human control over AI suggestions. It deliberately avoids government aesthetics, tourist imagery, excessive glass, heavy 3D, and unsupported performance or employment claims.

## Implementation consequence

The direction is implemented in small slices. Phase 17 currently includes semantic tokens, the homepage value story, and an abstract city story. Intro, template catalog, internal product surfaces, and full visual QA remain staged work and cannot be called complete until their evidence exists.
