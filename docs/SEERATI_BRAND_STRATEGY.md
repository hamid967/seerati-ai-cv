# Seerati Saudi Professional Intelligence

The proposed identity is modern Saudi, professional, human, calm, trustworthy, intelligent, and global across Arabic and English. It should serve graduates, employees, and leaders without looking like a government portal or a tourism campaign.

The palette uses Midnight Navy as the foundation, Saudi Emerald sparingly for success/positive action, Warm Sand and Pearl White for surfaces, Slate for secondary text, Sky Blue for AI/information, Amber for warnings, and red only for errors. Meaning must never depend on color alone.

The product promise remains factual: create and improve an Arabic or English CV, review advisory ATS signals, and keep guest data private by default. No unverified employment, ATS, government, or “best in Saudi Arabia” claim is permitted.

This document is a design direction, not evidence of user preference. Controlled beta research is required before selecting among Professional Minimal, Saudi Future, and Intelligent Workspace directions.
