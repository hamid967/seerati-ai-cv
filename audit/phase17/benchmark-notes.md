# Phase 17 Benchmark Notes

**Date:** 2026-08-13

## Sources reviewed

1. [Webflow hero templates](https://webflow.com/list/hero) — Webflow describes hero templates as an immediate message plus visual area and CTA; the page also exposes premium template pricing and feature descriptions. This is evidence of a common market pattern, not a license to copy any template.
2. [Made in Webflow storytelling](https://webflow.com/made-in-webflow/storytelling) — The community showcase emphasizes story-led pages, browseable examples, and cloneable content. Seerati must not clone these sites.
3. [Awwwards design agencies](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/design-agencies/) — The directory presents agency sites as narrative showcases with varied visual styles and interaction levels. It is useful for studying hierarchy and pacing only.
4. [Noomo Agency](https://noomoagency.com/) — The public page frames immersive work around narrative, craft, and a clear services/work/contact structure while listing recognitions. Seerati should use a lighter, performance-first version and make no award claims of its own.
5. [Apple Privacy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/) — Apple presents privacy as a product-level value and explains processing boundaries in plain language. Seerati can apply the principle of visible privacy explanations without copying wording or visual identity.
6. [Linear](https://linear.app/) — The public page connects product value to purpose-built workflows, speed, agent collaboration, and visible work states. Seerati can use progressive disclosure and visible status, not the interface or copy.
7. [Notion](https://www.notion.com/) — The page leads with a simple value proposition, AI capabilities, workflow examples, and trust signals. Seerati should keep claims evidence-based and privacy-specific.
8. [Kickresume](https://www.kickresume.com/en/) — The page combines resume creation, AI writing, templates, examples, mobile usage, and data-protection messaging. Marketing claims and user counts are not portable facts for Seerati.
9. [Enhancv](https://enhancv.com/) — Public positioning combines AI writing, ATS feedback, and job tailoring; this is a competitor claim, not an independent validation.
10. [Rezi](https://www.rezi.ai/) — Public positioning was reviewed as a resume/AI/ATS reference; no competitor superiority is inferred.
11. [Teal](https://www.tealhq.com/) — Public positioning was reviewed as a job-search/resume workflow reference; no competitor metrics are imported.

## Design deductions for Seerati

The original direction should combine one clear first-screen promise, a visible privacy boundary, a lightweight resume preview, progressive product storytelling, and deliberate CTA repetition. Motion must be subordinate to comprehension and must never block HTML, LCP, keyboard access, reduced-motion users, or mobile interaction.

The differentiator is not copying a Webflow aesthetic. It is a Saudi-first, Arabic-first, privacy-visible resume workflow with free creation and printing, optional registration, memory-only guest boundaries, transparent AI acceptance/rejection, and ATS guidance that is explicitly non-guaranteed.

## License and originality decision

No paid template, copied layout, external CSS/JS, third-party image, font, icon set, or animation asset is being imported from these sources. Any future asset must be recorded in an asset license register before use. CSS/SVG patterns will be authored inside the repository and kept abstract rather than reproducing recognizable marks, government logos, or landmarks.
